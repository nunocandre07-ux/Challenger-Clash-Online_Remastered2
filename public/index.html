📖 Challenger Clash Rulebook

Moves, effects, cooldowns, charges and clashes
⚔️ Core battle rules

🤖 Solo / Offline vs Bot: choose Bot HP and Special/Trait from the main menu. The CPU occupies Player 2 and automatically takes turns/reactions, so no second browser is needed.

  • 🎓 Tutorial Popups: use the clearly marked ON/OFF switch in the top-right of this Rulebook. It defaults ON the first time. When enabled, battle buttons stop before acting and show a short explanation. Continue performs the action; Cancel returns without using it. Timing-game HIT buttons are never interrupted mid-game.
  • Each champion starts with 50–100 HP. You may choose a value in that range or roll 50–100 when the battle begins.
  • Initiative is a server-side 50/50 roll.
  • Normal Attack: 3–5 base damage plus one Strike Timing bar. BAD gives no timing bonus, GOOD +1 damage, PERFECT +2. A natural maximum die roll is a Critical Hit for +1.
  • Heavy Attack: four timing bars. Each BAD = 1 damage, GOOD = 2, PERFECT = 3. Four PERFECT hits trigger a special Pan Perfect critical presentation. Heavy Attack has a 1-turn cooldown.
  • Defence → Guard: grants 4–6 Defence and stacks up to 20. Defence is secondary HP: incoming direct damage consumes Defence first, then any leftover damage hits HP. Example: 5 Defence vs 6 damage leaves 0 Defence and deals 1 HP damage. The 3/3 marker does not lock Guard.
  • Defence → Parry: stores a reactive stance for the next incoming attack/effect. When an attack arrives, you now get a visible ~1.3 second GET READY countdown before the timing bar appears. Parry is still extremely tight: even low-damage hits sweep quickly, and every full 3 points of incoming damage makes the marker faster and the success window smaller again. Success negates the hit/effect. Missing lets it through and causes +1 extra damage. Existing Bleed/Poison/Shock/Burn already on you cannot be parried when they tick.
  • Heavy vs Parry: Heavy Attack has four separate hits, so a defender in Parry Stance must complete four reaction bars—one for each hit.
  • Dice presentation: every gameplay D-roll is shown with a one-second die-throw animation before the visual result continues.
  • Charged Attack: requires stored Energy Charge, normally rolls D12, and uses the one-bar Strike Timing system.
  • Momentum: Attack-button attacks now gain an additional +8% on top of their existing timing gain, Beams gain 18%, damaging Special-menu attacks gain 7 base Momentum before character modifiers, and damaging Companion attacks also generate a small amount of Momentum and do not pay the normal -3 non-Attack Momentum tax. At 50% attacks gain +1 damage; at 100% they gain +2. At 100%, the fighter's Ultimate is also READY; using an Ultimate spends Momentum back to 0%.
🎲 Action
  • Action uses D20 LEFT vs D20 RIGHT. Current stats automatically add Advantage and Disadvantage. Final RIGHT must be strictly greater than LEFT; ties fail.
  • Using Action starts a 3-turn cooldown. A failed check ends the turn. A passed check is resolved, then the turn ends.
  • Action → Attack/Defence randomizes the dice count: D4 = 1–4 dice, D6 = 1–3, D8 = 1–2, and D10/D12/D20 = 1 die.
  • Action → Other Action shows a red dot when a contextual move exists, such as turning off Nightcrawler's Lights Out.
  • Item Shop: open it from the Action button. Buying an item does not require an Action check; instead, it immediately costs your entire turn. The Shop offers four random items at a time, refreshed after a purchase. The bought item is stored in My Attack list.
  • Companion Moves: every normal Companion gets its own separate Companion Move. After using all of its movement points, that Companion has a 1-personal-turn recovery cooldown before it can move again. Using it does not spend your normal move, so you can use a Companion ability and still Attack/Defend/etc. in the same turn. If you spend your normal move first, the turn stays open for the remaining Companion Move or you can end the turn. Zombie Dancers keep their special multi-Companion-move allowance.
  • If the opponent has a Companion, Action also shows Deal with opponent's Companion. LEFT rolls D20 and RIGHT rolls D20; the Companion dies only when RIGHT is strictly higher.
🔥 Charge, Beam & Beam Clash
  • Energy Charge takes a setup turn and then a completion turn. Stored Energy can power a Charged Attack or begin Beam Charge.
  • Beam Charge is a second charge stage. Once Beam is ready, it can be fired. A Beam always starts with +6 flat damage plus its normal D20 roll (before other bonuses/penalties). After firing, Beam is unavailable for the next 2 of that champion's turns.
  • A target with a ready or still-charging Beam may counter and begin a Beam Clash.
  • Before Beam Clash there is a 5-second ready countdown. The clash itself lasts 5 seconds: Player 1 mashes A, Player 2 mashes D.
  • Every full 5 points of Beam-damage difference gives the stronger Beam 1 starting click.
🗡️ Parry
  • Enter Parry Stance from Defence. The base window is already narrow, and each full 3 incoming damage makes the reaction faster and tightens the window again.
  • A successful Parry completely denies that incoming hit/effect and keeps Parry Stance ready, so you can continue successfully Parrying as many times as you can react.
  • If any Parry fails, the move lands with the existing +1 failed-Parry damage and Parry enters a 2-turn cooldown.
  • Heavy Attack still requires four separate Parry reactions. All four must succeed to keep the stance ready.
🛒 Items, Shop & My Attack list
  • Every item belongs to one shared item catalog. Items added to the game are available both in the Shop and as possible Magic Bag draws.
  • Buy through Action → Shop. You will see four random available items at a time; the offers refresh after each purchase. Buying skips your entire turn and stores the item. Use stored items from Attack → Use Item.
  • Portal Gun: summons one random Companion from Zombie, Kebab Stand, Baby, Dog, Cat, or Vaporeon. Teen and Adult are growth stages. Portal Gun is in the shared Shop/Magic Bag item catalog.
  • Zombie: Hold in Place (2-turn cooldown), Defend Me for 5 Companion Defence, and Bite, which inflicts D4 Poison damage for 3 turns.
  • Kebab Stand: produces a Kebab every 2 owner turns. Claim it from Companions. Kebab heals 4 HP and is also a normal Shop/Magic Bag item.
  • Baby: only Defend Me for 3 protection. After waiting 3 owner turns it grows into Teen.
  • Teen: Defend Me for 5. Gooning inflicts Armour Break, Weakness and PTSD for 3 turns; 6-turn cooldown. After 4 owner turns Teen grows into Adult.
  • PTSD: while active, you cannot use the same main battle button twice in a row. You must switch among Attack, Defence, Charge, Effects, Special, or Action. Forced Release Themselves is exempt so it cannot soft-lock a player.
  • Adult: Depression does nothing; Overtime gives +1 move for free once per turn; Groceries gives a random item (2-turn cooldown); Defend Me gives 7 protection.
  • Dog / Cat: Bite deals 3 damage with a 33% Bleed chance, usable once per turn without spending a move. Petting does nothing except play clapping sounds.
  • Vaporeon: Water Gun deals 3 Water damage (2-turn cooldown); Nightcrawler is weak to Water and takes ×1.5 damage. Seduce has a 4-turn cooldown and uses the existing 50% Seduced check with D4 duration.
  • Companion Defence: absorbs damage before normal Defence and blocks newly applied negative effects. If all Companion Defence is depleted, that defending Companion is lost.
  • National Flag: +25 percentage points of Momentum, maximum 2 uses per fighter. Health Potion: heals 5–10 HP.
  • Box of Chocolates: heals the opponent 5 HP, then has a 50% Seduced chance. On success, D4 decides duration. While Seduced, half of direct attack damage that champion deals is reflected back onto them.
  • Moustache: Confuses the opponent for their next turn, making their attacks and thrown Effects miss.
  • Hot Potato: starts a pass-back-and-forth minigame. Every pass has a 25% explosion chance. Explosion deals 6–7 damage and ends the game. A ready Parry can deflect the explosion and keep Hot Potato going.
  • Kebab: heals 4 HP. It is available from the Shop, Magic Bag, and Kebab Stand.
  • Truck: 15–20 damage. Magic Bag only; not sold in Shop.
  • Lion: summons Lion Companion with one Companion Move: Defend Me (10) or Big Bite (10 + Bleed).
  • Golden Apple: regenerates 10 HP for 3 personal turns. Shop price: 2 turns.
  • Anvil: horizontal timing minigame; BAD 5 / GOOD 10 / PERFECT 17 damage.
🧪 Effects
  • Shock: Nightcrawler Special only. Lasts 2 affected turns; deals D4 damage (D6 if powered by Strengthen) and makes a 50/50 skip-turn check. 5-turn cooldown.
  • Bleed: lasts 2 affected turns; deals D4 at turn start (D6 if powered by Strengthen). 5-turn cooldown.
  • Poison: lasts 2 affected turns and deals D4 at turn start. 5-turn cooldown.
  • Weakness: lasts 2 turns, lowers offensive dice, and now gives -2 on relevant Action attack stats instead of -1. 2-turn cooldown.
  • Effects: no cooldown on the main Effects button. Each individual effect still has its own cooldown. Effects applied from this button last one additional turn.
  • Shock: Shock has moved completely into Nightcrawler's Special menu. It no longer appears in the normal Effects button or Action → Effect. Its existing Shock mechanics and cooldown remain the same.
  • Armour Break: destroys 75% stored Defence immediately and prevents Defence moves on the target’s next turn. It boosts attacks against that target and now counts as +2/-2 on relevant Action stats instead of +1/-1. 2-turn cooldown.
  • Strengthen: upgrades the next offensive die one damage tier; an already-D20 roll receives +2 instead. It can stack with Armour Break. 2-turn cooldown.
⭐ Generated Specials
  • Standard: no Special.
  • Berserker: passive +1 attack damage at half HP or lower. Rage grants Strengthen; 5-turn Special cooldown.
  • Iron Wall: passive +1 Defence rolls. Fortify adds 5 Defence up to 20; 5-turn Special cooldown.
  • Arcane Surge: passive +1 on D12/D20 attacks and Beams. Channel instantly prepares Energy Charge; 5-turn Special cooldown.
  • Trickster: passive +1 to Action checks. Loaded Dice gives +4 to the next Action check; 5-turn Special cooldown.
🌑 Nightcrawler
  • Lights Out: stays active until the opponent passes Action → Other Action and chooses to turn the lights on. Nightcrawler gains +1 attack damage and +2 Defence. 5-turn cooldown.
  • Night Five: only usable during Lights Out. Gives two actions on each of Nightcrawler's next 2 turns. 7-turn cooldown.
👁️ Blue Judas
  • Six Eyes: active for the next 6 turns. Incoming attacks have a 1-in-3 chance to be completely blocked. Then Six Eyes enters a 7-turn cooldown. Each activation unlocks one I.V. attempt.
  • Infinity: costs 7 Cursed Energy and offers a tight, damage-scaled timing defence against the next three incoming attacks. Stronger hits tighten its timing window.
  • Max Red: retains its existing basic strike and charge bonuses until its full moveset is specified.
  • Max Blue: costs 5 Cursed Energy and uses Gravity Control. Stored Blue Charges give +2 damage each, with a random debuff for spending two charges.
  • Domain Expansion I.V.: can only start while Six Eyes is active. Starts at 0/3. Either charge for 3 turns at 4 Cursed Energy per turn while still acting, or spend 15 CE to activate on the next turn with a Domain Clash response window. A single 15+ HP hit while charging cancels it.
  • When I.V. activates, the opponent loses their next 3 turns and their UI is flooded with animated equations while trapped in the Domain.
  • Reverse Cursed Technique: costs 4 Positive Energy and heals 7 HP for three pulses.
👑 Red Judas
  • Dismantle: costs 4 Cursed Energy. Memorize five grey symbols chosen from seven possibilities over 3.5 seconds, then enter them in order within four seconds. Each correct position contributes to the final 3 / 5 / 8 / 12 / 15 damage scale; a mistake does not erase earlier correct symbols. Four or five correct apply Scored (+3 to the next Cleave).
  • Binding Vow: available only during King of Curses. Costs 5 current HP and immediately adds +1 move to the current turn. It does not consume a move. Maximum 4 uses per battle and 2 uses per turn. It can reduce Red Judas to 0 HP.
  • King of Curses: active for 5 turns, +5 Cursed Energy gained per owner turn, then a 7-turn cooldown. Offensive rolls get +2 damage and ignore stored Defence and block effects such as Six Eyes. Each activation unlocks one M.S. attempt. When it ends, the energy cap returns to 8 and all stored Cursed and Positive Energy is lost.
  • Domain Expansion M.S.: can only start during King of Curses. Choose three charging turns (spend 4 Cursed Energy each, continue using other moves), or spend 15 Cursed Energy to prepare activation on the next turn. Eligible opponents can challenge with a Domain Clash response. Charged Domains remain interruptible.
  • When M.S. activates, the battlefield becomes Blood Red, the victim's UI is covered in animated slash marks, and the opponent suffers Bleed and takes 5 Domain damage per affected turn for four turns. These effects do not skip turns.
  • Reversal Cursed Technique: costs 4 Positive Energy and restores 7 HP across three healing pulses.
🎬 Master of Presentation
  • Brainbot Swarm: replaces the current Companion with Brainbot. Brainbot has Hold in Place, Bite (same 3-turn Poison interpretation as Zombie Bite), and one-use Defend Me for 3 Companion Defence.
  • Black Mamba Armor: costs 15 Momentum, grants 10–15 Defence (cap 20) for 3 personal turns and unlocks Mamba Strike and Targeting Systems.
  • Targeting Systems: three seconds to tap distant targets across the screen. Always deals 4 damage plus 3 for each successful target.
  • Spider Bot: costs 10 Momentum, grants 5 Defence and one 25% dodge check against the next damaging attack. Unlocks Electricity Turret and Music Speakers for 3 turns.
  • Electricity Turret: Space Invaders-style shooter with 15 descending enemies. Use WASD and Space (touch buttons on phones). Deals 5 plus 1 damage per enemy shot down.
  • Music Speakers: applies Groovin for 3 opponent turns. A random main button is disabled each turn.
  • Mamba Strike: very-fast shrinking-ring target attack. The ring loops back outside if you wait too long. Damage zones are 5 / 10 / 13 / 17 from edge to bullseye. Requires Black Mamba Armor and has a 1-turn cooldown.
  • Decoy: every use adds one fake fighter UI. On the opponent's next Attack-button attack, Beam, or Mamba Strike, they must choose the real UI. One Decoy = 2 choices, two Decoys = 3, etc. A wrong choice makes that attack miss completely and consumes all stacked Decoys.
  • Death Ray: rotating-needle skill check inspired by the circular reference. Hit the small green wedge up to 5 times. Every success adds +1 damage to the next Beam, reverses needle direction, and halves the next green wedge. The needle/dial glow green while inside the accepted zone, and presses up to 10% of the wedge width beyond either edge still count. Missing ends the calibration but keeps the bonus already earned. Hitting all 5 sets Beam READY and grants a one-use cooldown bypass so it can fire immediately even while Beam cooldown is active. 2-turn cooldown.
  • The Difference Is Presentation: chain three different Master of Presentation abilities (including Brainbot abilities, Mamba Strike and Decoy) to gain +10 bonus Momentum, then the chain resets.
🧤 The Gloved One
  • Dance Battle trait: The Gloved One gets one extra miss allowance; their opponent gets one fewer. Base 5 becomes 6 for The Gloved One and 4 for the opponent. If both players are The Gloved One, both return to 5.
  • Moon Walk: a five-note solo rhythm challenge that is much faster and tighter than Dance Battle. A note becomes hittable as soon as it reaches roughly the first 20% of the bottom receptor box. Each hit adds 2 raw damage; each miss subtracts 1. Final damage cannot go below 0. 5-turn cooldown.
  • The Kid Is Not My Son!: 4-turn cooldown; clears active bad status effects including Shock, Bleed, Poison, Burn, Syringe damage, Marbles, Seduced, Confused, Weakness, Armour Break, Exhaustion and PTSD.
  • Beat It: a solo ten-note falling-arrow rhythm performance. Match ← ↓ ↑ → when each note reaches the receptor; notes speed up with each hit and reset after a miss. Each successful note deals +1 damage. 1-turn cooldown.
  • Thriller Night: passive, once per battle. The first time HP reaches 0, The Gloved One survives at 1 HP and cannot die for their next 3 full personal turns. The arena becomes much darker with large flashing stage lights for the entire Thriller Night. Zombie Dancers replace the current Companion. When the third full turn ends, The Gloved One dies instantly.
  • Zombie Dancers — Hold in Place: can stack. Every stack requires a separate Release Themselves move from the opponent.
  • Zombie Dancers — Defend Me: gives +5 stackable Companion Defence. It can be used 3 times total. Each use permanently reduces future Zombie Dancer moves per turn from 3 → 2 → 1; the third use destroys the Dancers.
👜 The Great Exception
  • Passive drawback: normal number-roll dice are 2 sides smaller: D20→D18, D12→D10, D10→D8, D8→D6, D6→D4 and D4→D2. Magic Bag item rolls are exempt.
  • Umbrella: for the opponent's next 2 attack windows, Basic Attacks automatically miss because The Great Exception is too far away. It also stores one 50/50 dodge against the next other incoming attack. 7-turn cooldown.
  • Ultimate — Reality Manipulation: at 100% Momentum, choose HP rupture (10–22, nonlethal), Momentum drain (20–50), or a temporary main-button seal (1–3 turns). Both players compete in a four-pulse Reality Clash to determine the strength. Upgraded Magic Bag lasts 2 turns afterward.
  • Magic Bag: draws from the complete shared Shop/item catalog. Only the drawing player sees the item popup. After an item is actually used, that exact item is excluded from Magic Bag for that player's next 2 personal turns; then it returns to the pool. Discarding it does not create the lockout. Magic Bag itself has no cooldown.
  • Items include Piano, BAZOOKA, Marbles, Basketball, Football, Marble, Knife, Cupcake, Birthday Cake, Used Syringe, Dice, Garbage, Lollipop, Toy Army, Plates, Hot Head Burrito and Shield.
  • Dice item: adds an item D6 to the next Attack or Defence roll. Hot Head Burrito: D20 heal + Burn (3 damage for 2 turns). Toy Army: D20 damage + Armour Break.
🔄 Pokémon Trainer
  • Pokémon Rotation: choose your first Pokémon at the start and choose again only when your active Pokémon faints. You can freely switch a healthy Pokémon at any point from Pokémon Moves without spending your action. Fainted Pokémon cannot be selected.
  • Limited PP: Pokémon moves use PP instead of ordinary cooldowns. The remaining PP is shown during selection and in the Special menu.
  • Pokémon PP: each Pokémon has limited PP per move. Charizard has exactly 1 PP for each move. Switching is free and does not require a fresh pick each turn.
  • Shared Pokémon HP: damage that reaches the Trainer’s real HP also damages the active Pokémon by the same amount. Fainted Pokémon stay unavailable until PokéCenter heals them.
  • PokéCenter: replaces the Special button. Choose either heal one Pokémon to full or restore +1 PP to every move for exactly two distinct chosen Pokémon. Either option ends the full turn.
  • Vaporeon: Water Gun deals 1–6 Water damage and Wet for 2 turns; Wet reduces Defence gained from rolls by 2. Seduce has a 50% chance to inflict Seduced for D4 turns.
  • Pikachu — Offensive pressure: Quick Attack builds Momentum; Thunderbolt can inflict Shock; Volt Tackle deals 5 self-damage as recoil.
  • Snorlax — Defense: while active, direct incoming damage is reduced by 2. Block builds Defence and punishes the next direct attacker; Rest heals and adds Defence.
  • Gengar — Disruption: Shadow Ball can inflict Weakness, Hypnosis can steal the opponent’s next turn, and Toxic Trick applies Poison.
  • Charizard — Heavy damage: Flamethrower and Fire Blast can Burn; Dragon Rush can inflict Armour Break. Every Charizard move has only 1 PP.
  • Team Rocket (Ultimate): at 100 Momentum, restore all five Pokémon to full HP and PP and randomly steal one enemy Special Attack for three turns. The stolen attack temporarily replaces the Trainer’s Ultimate button, while the opponent cannot use that specific Special. Plays the Team Rocket cinematic.
🌀 Domain Clash
  • A Domain cannot be started while another Domain is already active.
  • If one Domain activates while the other is charging at 2/2, a Domain Clash begins. If the other is only 0/2 or 1/2, that unfinished Domain collapses.
  • There is a 3-second GET READY countdown, followed by a 10-second Domain Clash.
  • One random number (1–4) appears at a time. Press that matching keyboard number. Correct inputs push your Domain colour forward; wrong inputs do nothing.
  • The first player to complete the sequence wins immediately. If time expires first, the player farther ahead wins; an exact tie collapses both Domains.
  • I.V. uses turquoise. M.S. uses blood red.
🔁 Rooms & ending a match
  • Player 1 creates a room code and starts the battle after both players lock in.
  • Funny Business / Infinite Ultimates: Player 1 can toggle optional matchup cinematics and, separately, a fun mode that lets implemented Ultimates be used anytime without needing 100% Momentum. Infinite Ultimates is OFF by default.
  • Battle music: the game generates an original arcade/synthwave battle theme in the browser with drums, bass, chord stabs and arpeggios. Music Off hard-mutes battle music and Domain Clash music; sound effects such as Bite of 87!? remain audible.
  • Either player may use End Battle during a match. This ends the fight without declaring a winner.
  • After a battle, Player 1 may rematch the same champions or send both players back to champion select.
